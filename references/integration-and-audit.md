# Integration, audits and publication delivery

These stages are **designed protocols**; they must not be represented as field-proven merely because earlier writer pilots passed.

## Neighbor integration

Read actual adjacent locked passages and interface contracts. Detect duplicate explanations, inconsistent terms, circular or missing transitions, pronoun/referent breaks, competing factual claims and prematurely introduced conclusions. Propose minimal changes while preserving mature prose and original author voice. Any change to a lock requires approval and a new version. A complete interface map is not a substitute for reading adjacent text.

## Whole-document structural review

Evaluate chapter thesis, progression, section weight, gaps, author-position consistency, contested issues, necessity of tables/figures and non-repetitive conclusions. Distinguish global defects that require re-architecture from local copyediting. No free-form model-wide rewrite without an approved change plan.

## Separate audit tracks

- **Source and legal audit**: exact claims/authority/date/attribution and verified page/article; unverified precise locators remain open until resolved or removed. Never invent footnote pages.
- **Language audit**: natural disciplinary language, excessive formulaic transitions, over-defense, unnecessary redefinition, and preservation of mature original wording. Keep genuine legal safeguards and disagreements.
- **Document fidelity audit**: full inventory accounting; table and caption position; footnote anchor matching; image/equation inclusion; changed tracked edits; Word styles, pagination and typography.
- **Version audit**: every incorporated unit points to an approved exact hash and documented authorizations.

## Delivery

Produce the requested editable and/or publication formats only after content, source and fidelity gates pass. Keep a version manifest, unresolved-items list, editorial signoff checklist and final deliverable paths. Do not declare the manuscript publication-ready solely because all sections are locked. Final approval belongs to the user/publisher as appropriate.
