# Research and decisions

Start from specific writing dependencies and competing hypotheses, not a mandate to prove a favored author's numerical or doctrinal proposal. Set discovery boundaries: jurisdiction, period, primary/legal source first where appropriate, author attribution, full-text versus abstract and explicit exclusions. Record source access failures, partial reads and contradictory evidence.

## Fact and source ledger

For each material claim record at least: `CLAIM_ID`, `TYPE`, `CLAIM`, `SOURCE_ID`, `PRIMARY_OR_SECONDARY`, `LOCATOR`, `READ_SCOPE`, `CHECKED_AT`, `CONFIDENCE`, `LIMITATIONS`. Useful types: `LAW_FACT`, `HISTORICAL_FACT`, `EMPIRICAL_FACT`, `AUTHOR_POSITION`, `SOURCE_METADATA`, `NORMATIVE_PROPOSAL`. Draft prose must not upgrade a paper abstract into full-text evidence or turn an author's proposed law into enacted law.

## Decision request

A disputed substantive rule becomes a structured Decision Request rather than a discretionary Codex choice. Include: (1) exact question; (2) user's or relevant author's verified position; (3) current law and factual constraints; (4) strongest support, contrary evidence and unknowns; (5) options with genuine trade-offs; (6) consequences for writing units and interfaces; (7) whether further research is likely to resolve uncertainty; (8) recommended decision, clearly marked as advice; (9) proposed words to allow/prohibit in prose.

When a threshold or specific parameter lacks direct support, do not manufacture evidence by comparing unlike numerical values. It may remain an expressly owned policy judgment, be changed, or be deferred. All choices require approval by the authorized decision-maker.

## Stopping research

Close research as `RESEARCHED` when the credible identified evidence and its limits suffice for an informed normative choice, even if uncertainty remains. Record what was not obtained; do not claim exhaustive absence of scholarship merely because search returned zero. Reopen only for a specific material gap or new reliable evidence.
